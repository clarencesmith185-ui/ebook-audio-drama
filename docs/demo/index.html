Transcript

Rain tapped the café window. “The last train leaves at nine,” Maya said. “Then we still have time,” Eli replied, setting down his cup.

How it was made

Narrator, Maya and Eli use original Qwen3-TTS VoiceDesign references and Base synthesis. Rain was generated with TangoFlux. The rain bed is set 18 dB below overall speech RMS before additional speech ducking. No train sound is added; the train is only mentioned in dialogue.

Unreviewed experimental draft. Source reconstruction, saved voice bindings, finite samples, headroom and MP3 decoding passed technical checks. Audible word accuracy, voice quality and the rain sound have not been listening-approved.

No private book text, private voice references or cloned real-person recording is included.
